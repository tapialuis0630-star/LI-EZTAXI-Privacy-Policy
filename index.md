# LI EZ TAXI Passenger App – Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 11, 2026**

LI EZ TAXI ("we", "us", or "our") operates the LI EZ TAXI Passenger mobile application.

## Information We Collect

When you use the LI EZ TAXI Passenger App, we may collect:

- Your name
- Your phone number
- Your precise location when needed to provide transportation services
- Pickup and destination information
- Information related to your requested taxi ride

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Process and manage taxi requests
- Connect passengers with available drivers
- Provide pickup and transportation services
- Calculate trip distance and fares
- Display the driver's location during an active ride
- Communicate with passengers regarding their ride
- Maintain and improve the service

## Location Information

The app may use precise location information to provide transportation services, including determining pickup locations and displaying the driver's location during an active ride.

## Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information necessary to provide a taxi service may be transmitted to our transportation system and made available to the assigned driver, such as your name, phone number, pickup location, destination, and ride information.

## Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect information transmitted through the app. Data transmitted between the app and our services is encrypted in transit.

## Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information as necessary to provide and maintain our services. If you would like to request deletion of your personal information, contact us using the contact information associated with LI EZ TAXI.

## Children's Privacy

The LI EZ TAXI Passenger App is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

## Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or requests regarding your personal information, please contact LI EZ TAXI.
